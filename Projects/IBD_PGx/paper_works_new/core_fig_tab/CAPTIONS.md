## Figures

**Figure 1. Patient eligibility flow chart.**
Anti-TNF inhibitors included infliximab, adalimumab, and ustekinumab.
Patients without available anti-TNF concentration measurements or without
whole-genome sequencing data were excluded to define the analytic cohort
(n = 139). Patients with infliximab administration records formed the
infliximab population pharmacokinetic (PopPK) modeling cohort (n = 98).
One additional patient was excluded from the pharmacogenomic analyses
because the corresponding sequencing sample was removed during genotype
quality control (n = 97). Analyses were performed separately for the
overall treatment period, the induction phase, and the maintenance phase.
*Screening-stage counts (n = X,XXX) to be completed from the EMR
extraction.*
`Figure1_eligibility_flowchart.png / .pdf` — from `08_figure1_flowchart.py`

**Figure 2. Structural model diagram.**
Integrated intravenous and subcutaneous two-compartment model with
first-order absorption and first-order elimination (ADVAN4 TRANS4) used
to describe infliximab pharmacokinetics.
`Figure2_structural_model.png` — carried over from the previous version

**Figure 3. Infliximab clearance by TNFRSF1B rs1061622 genotype.**
Individual model-estimated clearance (CL) by genotype in the maintenance
phase (left) and over the overall treatment period (right). Circles
represent individual patients; horizontal bars and whiskers denote the
geometric mean and its 95% confidence interval. The y-axis is on a
logarithmic scale. Brackets show the adjusted geometric mean ratio (GMR)
with 95% confidence interval and the FDR-adjusted q value for the
recessive comparison (GG versus TT + TG), from ANCOVA on log-transformed
CL adjusted for sex, body weight, serum albumin, and anti-drug antibody
status.
`Figure3_CL_by_rs1061622.png / .pdf` — from `05_figure_cl_by_genotype.py`

## Tables

**Table 1. Baseline characteristics of the analytic and infliximab
cohorts.**
Categorical variables are presented as n (%) and continuous variables as
mean (standard deviation). Laboratory values are the first available
measurement per patient. TL, trough level.
`Table1_baseline_characteristics.csv` — from `01_table1_demographics.py`

**Table 2. Base and final population pharmacokinetic model parameter
estimates.**
Values are estimates with relative standard error (%RSE) in parentheses
and shrinkage in square brackets. Bootstrap results are medians with
5th–95th percentiles. CL, clearance; Vc, central volume of distribution;
Vp, peripheral volume of distribution; Q, intercompartmental clearance;
F1, relative bioavailability; Ka, absorption rate constant; IIV,
interindividual variability; ADA, anti-drug antibody.
*Note: these estimates predate the body-weight data correction;
re-estimation is pending.*
`Table2_popPK_parameters.csv` — transcribed in `07_core_tables.py`

**Table 3. Characteristics of the candidate variants in the
pharmacogenomic analysis cohort (n = 97).**
Genotype counts are given for each genotype in the coded-allele order.
Variants with a minor allele frequency (MAF) below 0.05 or a
Hardy–Weinberg equilibrium (HWE) exact test P value below 0.05 were
excluded from the association analyses. Variants without homozygous
carriers of the coded allele could not be evaluated under the recessive
model.
`Table3_variant_characteristics.csv` — from `07_core_tables.py`

**Table 4. Association between candidate variants and infliximab
clearance over the overall treatment period.**
Analysis of covariance on log-transformed clearance adjusted for sex,
body weight, serum albumin, and anti-drug antibody status. Effect
estimates are geometric mean ratios (GMRs) with 95% confidence
intervals. P values were adjusted for multiple comparisons within each
genetic model using the Benjamini–Hochberg false discovery rate
procedure (11 variants under the recessive model, 14 under the dominant
model). No association reached the prespecified threshold of q < 0.05.
`Table4_CL_association_overall.csv` — from `07_core_tables.py`

**Table 5. TNFRSF1B rs1061622 and infliximab clearance across analysis
periods (recessive model).**
Geometric mean clearance values are shown for GG homozygotes and for
patients with TT or TG genotypes, with adjusted geometric mean ratios
from ANCOVA on log-transformed clearance. Results from the corresponding
original-scale analysis and the Shapiro–Wilk test of residual normality
are shown for comparison.
`Table5_rs1061622_across_periods.csv` — from `07_core_tables.py`
